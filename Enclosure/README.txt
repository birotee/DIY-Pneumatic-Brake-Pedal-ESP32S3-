JACK PEDAL - 108 x 108 x 50 mm OVERALL

Base: 101 x 101 x 47 mm. Lid: 108 x 108 x 11 mm.
The lid overlaps by 8 mm, giving 50 mm assembled height.
3 mm walls/floor/lid top. Slip-over lid, 0.5 mm clearance per side.
Secure lid with tape or an external strap; this is not a snap fit.

BOARD FIT
Expansion board assumed 83 x 83 mm, placed on four 4 mm raised supports.
Use insulating pads as needed and narrow cable ties through floor slots.
Do not tie across components or antenna. No fixed screw posts: hole spacing unknown.
ADS1220 (21 x 21 x 8 mm) mounts UNDER THE LID in the 23 mm wide cradle.
Use a narrow tie through the lid slots to retain it, plus thin insulating pad.
Orient lid so ADC avoids tall connectors on the expansion board.
Internal clearance above expansion-board supports is 40 mm away from ADC.
Reserve 12 mm below lid for ADC/pad/tie, leaving 28 mm locally above supports.
Earlier 60 mm assembled wiring WILL NOT FIT unchanged. Rearrange and measure.
USB notch is 62 mm wide and starts 12 mm above exterior bottom.
Check USB plug height/reach and wire bend clearance before full printing.
Sensor notch is 12 mm wide and starts 25 mm above exterior bottom.

PRINT
STLs use mm. Print base floor-down and lid exterior-face-down as supplied.
Suggested starting settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 4 top/bottom layers,
15-20% infill. Designed without supports in supplied orientations.
Nonconductive filament only. Not water/dust sealed. Fit/thermal tests pending.
Both meshes passed closed-edge tests; physical fit is NOT guaranteed.
Print a partial-height base first to check board footprint and tie locations.
